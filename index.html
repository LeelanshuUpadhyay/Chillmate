THE PROBLEM

A MASSIVE MARKET. A STAGNANT CATEGORY.

01 1.4B+ CONSUMERS

India's 1.4 billion consumers face a hydration product gap that no single brand has adequately addressed.

Students on hot campuses carry basic bottles with no cooling. Office professionals lack a companion that integrates with their lifestyle. Travelers and gym-goers juggle multiple separate devices — a bottle, a fan, a speaker and a medicine box — when one intelligent product could replace them all.

Existing products offer two extremes: inexpensive, featureless bottles that do little beyond holding water, or imported premium products that are either prohibitively expensive or lack the combination of features Indian users need.

ChillMate is built to fill that gap.
